# 1:1 — Tomas, 2026-07-06

Fortnightly. 30 minutes.

## Leave

Confirmed the dates. **Out 20 July to 10 August**, parental leave. Second child, due
around the 24th. He has known since March and told me in April; I have not put it
anywhere the team can see it and that is my mistake, because it is now a constraint on
the migration and nobody planning the migration knows about it except him and me.

He raised it at the go/no-go on 25 June and I said we would deal with it at the next
one, which was the right call in the meeting and is now three weeks of nobody planning
around it.

Action for me: get the dates into the team calendar today. Not the reason, just the
dates.

## The cutover

He is uncomfortable. Not about the technical readiness — he says the rollback
rehearsal is the only thing he genuinely cares about and it is on the list. He is
uncomfortable that a 13 July cutover puts the entire two-week dual-write safety period
inside his absence, with Daniel as primary and Ravi, who has been on the rotation for
two weeks, as secondary.

His words: "I do not mind being unavailable for something that has gone well. I mind
being unavailable for the two weeks where we find out."

I asked whether he would rather it moved to before he goes or after he is back. Before,
clearly — he wants to be in the room for the flip. That means the 27th at the latest,
because after that he is gone.

I am going to let Mei arrive at the date herself. If I hand it to her as "Tomas's
constraint" it becomes a favour instead of a plan, and she has better reasons of her
own — the six-minute lag is real.

## Helios

He brought it up before I did, which I appreciated. He knows he cannot do Helios and
the migration and he would rather say it than have it said to him. We will take it to
next Tuesday's sync as a trade, not a debate.

He asked whether pausing it would be read as his project failing. I said no, and that
I would frame it as a resourcing trade in the sync so it is on the record that way.

## Him

Tired. He was on call for INC-2026-0617 and has carried four of its seven action
items since, and the second child is due in under three weeks. He has not taken a full day off since April.

I told him the cutover happens before he leaves or after he is back, and either way it
is not his problem to solve from parental leave. I meant it and I want to be held to
it — if the 27th goes badly, the instinct to call him will be strong.
