# How I want to run this team

Draft. I will turn this into the handbook once people have argued with it.

## Meetings

We are in ten time zones and there is exactly one hour where everyone is awake at
once: 13:00–14:00 UTC. It is 21:00 for Mei. I am not going to spend it.

So: one meeting nobody skips (Platform Sync, Tuesdays 09:00 UTC), one that is
biweekly and deep (Architecture Review), and everything else is written.

**Pre-reads are not optional.** If there is no document 24 hours before an
architecture or decision meeting, I will cancel the meeting. I have sat through too
many hours of people reading slides at each other. I read pre-reads first thing in my
morning and I will have comments in the doc before 10:00 UTC.

## Decisions

An ADR within two working days or the decision did not happen. I do not care about
the format. I care that in October somebody can find out why we did this.

I also want the objection in the ADR, in the objector's words, not paraphrased by the
person who won. Daniel is going to be wrong about something this year and I want to
be able to tell whether we heard him.

## What I have noticed about people already

Three weeks in, so this is provisional.

- **Daniel** thinks in writing and argues in writing. If you bring him a whiteboard
  he goes quiet, and people read that as agreement. It is not agreement.
- **Mei** will not say a number she cannot defend. When she says "I do not know" she
  means it and it is usually the most important thing said in the meeting.
- **Tomas** is the only person here who has been woken up at 3 a.m. by this system.
  Weight his operational objections accordingly.
- **Ava** moves fast and commits early. She is also the fastest to change her mind
  when she gets a number, which is rarer than moving fast.
- **Karen** is treated as "the QA person" and is actually the person with the clearest
  view of where the seams are.
- **Sofia** needs dates in writing. She has been burned by verbal dates.
- **Elliot** is new to the domain and asks the question everyone assumed was settled.
  Protect that.

## What I am worried about

We have three big things starting at once — the service split, a possible datastore
change, and a new public API — and four senior people. Something is going to slip and
I would rather choose which.
